Pharmacy Information

Our pharmacy provides medicines, healthcare products, prescription processing, and pharmacy-related services.

Prescription Medicines

Some medicines require a valid prescription before they can be ordered.
Customers can upload a prescription through the pharmacy system.
A pharmacist reviews uploaded prescriptions.
A prescription may be approved or rejected after pharmacist review.

Orders

Customers can place orders for available products.
Products that are inactive cannot be ordered.
Orders requiring prescription medicines must include an approved prescription.
Orders cannot be placed when sufficient stock is unavailable.

Safety

The AI assistant provides general pharmacy and product information.
The AI assistant does not diagnose medical conditions.
The AI assistant does not prescribe medicines.
The AI assistant does not recommend changing medication dosage.
Prescription approval is performed by a pharmacist, not by the AI assistant.

Customer Support

Customers can use the pharmacy system to view products, check order information, upload prescriptions, and communicate with the pharmacy assistant.
